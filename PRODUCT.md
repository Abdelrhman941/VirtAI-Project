# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Professionals, students, and general users seeking an intelligent, multimodal conversational agent. They interact with VirtAI in contexts ranging from casual chat to deep document analysis (RAG) and voice interaction.

## Product Purpose
VirtAI serves as an advanced virtual assistant. It aims to reduce friction in information retrieval, document understanding, and multimodal interaction (voice, vision, text) by providing a fast, highly-responsive, and accurate AI chat interface.

## Brand Personality
Intelligent, polished, unobtrusive, and highly capable. The aesthetic should evoke professional tools (like Obsidian or Linear) rather than playful consumer apps.

## Anti-references
- Bloated "AI Wrapper" apps with generic Tailwind templates.
- Disjointed, badly-spaced markdown rendering.
- Bright, low-contrast, or inaccessible dark modes.
- "Ghost card" aesthetics (using heavy blurs and oversized borders indiscriminately).

## Design Principles
1. **Content First:** The AI's response is the most important element; the UI should frame it, not compete with it.
2. **Dense but Readable:** Typography should balance information density with absolute clarity across scripts (Latin & Arabic).
3. **Flawless Fundamentals:** Core interactions (scrolling, text rendering, markdown parsing) must feel native and perfectly executed without jank or layout shifts.
4. **Accessible Contrast:** Colors must meet WCAG standards while maintaining a sleek, muted dark-mode elegance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Full bidirectional (Bidi) text support for seamless LTR and RTL (Arabic) rendering.
- WCAG AA compliant contrast ratios for text and interactive elements.
- Respect for `prefers-reduced-motion` settings.
